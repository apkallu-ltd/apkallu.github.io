## What the AI Coach Actually Does

When you open Ascend, the AI Coach asks you one question: *"What are you working on?"*

You answer however feels natural. *"I want to build a morning routine but I always give up by week two."* Or *"I need to start exercising but I only have 20 minutes and no equipment."* Or *"I want to get better sleep but I am on my phone until midnight."*

The AI follows up with a small number of short questions about your schedule, your experience, and what has made it difficult before. Not a form. A conversation.

Then it builds a programme — shaped around your specific answers.

*"Sounds like you need something that starts low-friction enough to survive the first two weeks. I have put together a 21-day morning routine that starts at 10 minutes a day and scales only when you are ready. Want to see it?"*

You tap yes. You see your actual tasks for Day 1. You join with one tap. Your first task is due tomorrow morning. You have gone from zero to committed in under 90 seconds.

## Why a Plan Built for You Hits Differently

A plan built around your schedule and your constraints removes the friction that kills most habit attempts before they start.

When the tasks already match your reality from Day 1, you do not have to adapt the plan to your life as you go. It was already adapted. That single difference — between a plan you are trying to fit into your life and a plan already fitted to it — is what makes the first two weeks survivable instead of exhausting.

The result is a programme that actually belongs to you. Not a template. Not someone else's routine that you borrowed and hoped for the best.

## Your Plan, Inside the App You Know

Once the AI builds your programme, it lives in Ascend exactly like any other plan. Your daily calendar shows your tasks at the times that made sense when you had the conversation. You check things off, build your streak, and track your progress day by day.

And at every milestone, you earn a badge.

## Badges That Mark Real Progress

Every programme built by the AI Coach comes with a set of badges tied to your actual milestones — thresholds that match the structure of your specific plan.

Your first badge is for showing up. For committing. For starting. That alone puts you ahead of everyone who had the same idea and never acted on it.

From there, milestone badges mark the moments that matter — the end of your first week, the halfway point, the final push. The completion badge is the one you work the whole programme to earn. When it arrives, it is a permanent record of something you actually did.
